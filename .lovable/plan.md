# Sree Cosmetology premium clinic website

## What I’ll build
- A polished, mobile-first clinic website with a calm medical aesthetic: warm white surfaces, charcoal typography, muted teal accents, restrained gold details, generous spacing, and subtle motion.
- Clear first-screen positioning for hair, skin, cosmetology, hair transplantation, Rajahmundry location, and consultation actions.
- A sticky desktop header, compact mobile navigation, floating WhatsApp action, and fixed mobile Call / WhatsApp / Book bar.
- Conversion-focused sections for concerns, hair transplant options, hair loss, non-surgical care, skin treatments, clinic strengths, treatment journeys, team, gallery, process, FAQs, location, and final enquiry prompt.

## Content integrity
- Keep phone, WhatsApp, hours, Google profile URL, doctors, qualifications, reviews, ratings, before/after photos, and clinic gallery as clearly marked editable placeholders until verified.
- Never publish invented testimonials, clinician credentials, results, prices, awards, statistics, or medical guarantees.

- Avoid showing fake before/after cases or fake clinical photographs; their areas will use polished replacement-ready states.

## Site structure
- Create focused pages for Home, Hair, Skin, Hair Transplant, Treatments, Before & After, About, Reviews, FAQs, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and Disclaimer.
- Keep a concise, conversion-led home page that previews the main services; deeper pages provide dedicated, shareable information.

## Visual assets
- Generate a cohesive set of premium, realistic clinic and consultation photographs for general presentation only.
- Clearly separate illustrative clinic imagery from evidence-based areas such as doctors, patient reviews, and before/after results.
- Optimize and lazy-load supporting images while prioritizing the first-screen image.

## Booking and contact behavior

## Search and accessibility

- Use semantic headings, descriptive image text, keyboard-accessible navigation/accordions/lightbox, visible focus states, and motion reduction support.
